How theming works

Every colour is an OKLCh custom property declared in src/styles/main.css, under :root for light and .dark for dark.

The accent presets above rewrite --primary, --chart-1, and --ring at runtime, picking a lightness that suits the current mode.

Choices persist to localStorage and reapply before first paint, so there is no flash of the wrong theme.
